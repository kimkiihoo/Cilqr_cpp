英文正文（仅限B201；分项均为每个规划周期平均耗时）
To examine the computational cost in more detail, the B201 dynamic obstacle avoidance scenario was rerun with timing instrumentation using the original benchmark implementations and settings (N = 80 and Δt = 0.1 s) on an Intel Core i7-12700H processor. All 400 planning cycles per method were retained, and the returned control sequences and predicted trajectories matched the uninstrumented benchmark exactly. R-CILQR and AL-iLQR required an average of 1.445 and 8.580 inner iterations per planning cycle, with measured mean inner-iteration times of 1.329 and 1.928 ms, respectively. Their mean per-cycle times for model linearization, cost-derivative evaluation, backward recursion, and forward rollout/line search were 0.017/0.107, 0.368/4.205, 0.060/0.382, and 1.092/10.427 ms, respectively (R-CILQR/AL-iLQR). Additional cost evaluation and native logging outside the forward pass took 0.700/1.578 ms, while the remaining per-cycle overhead was 0.031/0.169 ms, giving total mean planning times of 2.268/16.868 ms. AL-iLQR completed every cycle within its first outer iteration, with no multiplier updates. Thus, the observed runtime difference in this scenario is associated with fewer inner iterations and lower measured iteration cost, rather than repeated outer-loop updates. These measurements retain the original native logging and AL-iLQR parallel implementation and are specific to the tested implementation and hardware.

可选SQP补充句（若希望三方法都给出剖析）
For SQP-NMPC, the average number of major iterations was 10.870, and the mean planning time was 490.281 ms. Objective-gradient evaluation and the SLSQP core accounted for 448.745 and 21.418 ms per planning cycle, respectively. The amortized minimization time was 45.088 ms per major iteration; this quantity is not a directly measured iLQR inner-iteration time.

中文含义
本次以原版B201设置重新运行，在同一批400周期中核实迭代次数和分项耗时。R平均1.445次内迭代，AL平均8.580次；平均实际单次内迭代耗时为1.329和1.928 ms。AL每周期均只运行1次外层，没有乘子更新。因此B201不能用于证明“多次外层更新造成了时间差”，应将结论写成：该场景中R迭代更少，测得的单次迭代开销也更低。
